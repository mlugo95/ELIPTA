  • What is ELIPTA?

    ELIPTA stands for "Electronic Local Interface for Providersand Treatment Assessment".


    An Electronic Health Record designed by physicians for physicians.

  • Our Goal

    To facilitate electronic user interface and experience customized to the exact specifications of physician providers in their private practice. We aim to intuitively guide the navigation of electronic information through the perspective of a physician in a simple, yet efficient, and timely manner.

  • Background

    The need for a physician-tailored approach to simplicity and efficiency from an EHR inspired the development of ELIPTA. The only modern EHR designed and built from scratch by a physician to satisfy the needs for information requests and navigation through the lens of a physician.

  • Company Overview

    Navideon Inc. Specialists in healthcare software technology, development, and multi-platform deployment in the United States.
